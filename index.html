Ranjan Bassi

Hey there! I am a PhD researcher working on computational nanoplasmonics and light-matter interaction, with a focus on time-dependent density functional theory (TDDFT), particularly real-time TDDFT, and ultrafast electron dynamics at the nanoscale. I am currently based at CINaM (CNRS, Aix-Marseille University), where my research explores optical response, plasmonic properties, and other light-induced phenomena in metallic nanoclusters and quantum materials.
